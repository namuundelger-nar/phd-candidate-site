Thesis & Articles

Bachelor's Thesis: "One Belt, One Road": Evolution, Challenges and Prospects for International Cooperation

RANEPA, Moscow · 2025 · Supervisor: Prof. Alexander Ivanovich Sukharev, Dr. Sc.

An analysis of China's Belt and Road Initiative — its historical evolution from the ancient Silk Road, its implementation across Central Asia, the China–Mongolia–Russia economic corridor and Mongolia's "Steppe Road" project, and the initiative's economic risks, soft-power dimension, and geopolitical prospects.

Hypothesis: the One Belt, One Road initiative will strengthen international cooperation and economic integration in the region, despite existing geopolitical challenges, provided that participating countries ensure effective governance and coordination.
